Beneath Puget Sound: two layers of earthquakes

A west-to-east slice of the ground, seen from the side. It shows all 990 earthquakes M ≥ 2.5 recorded between 46.5°N and 48.8°N since 1980, with quarry blasts removed. Shallow quakes break the North American crust. Deeper ones are inside the Juan de Fuca Plate as it sinks eastward, where the 1949, 1965 and 2001 quakes struck.
